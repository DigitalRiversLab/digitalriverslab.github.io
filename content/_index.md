The Digital Rivers Lab studies the quantity and quality of water in rivers and lakes, from individual river reaches to global patterns. We combine satellite remote sensing, low-cost sensing technology, and deep learning models. Our aim is to use these observational and predictive tools to help monitor water resources, manage infrastructure, and protect ecosystems.

We are located in the **School of Computing** and the **Department of Geology & Geophysics** at the University of Wyoming.
