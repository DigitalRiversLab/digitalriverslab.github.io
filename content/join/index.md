### Getting in touch

***Note:*** While I support the use and development of AI for many general and modeling tasks, I find the use of generative AI in personal communication unacceptable. If you are contacting me, I want to hear from **you**! 

Email [Ted Langhorst](mailto:ted.langhorst@uwyo.edu) with:

- The research questions or topics that excite you.
- Your background and relevant experience (research, fieldwork, electronics, coding).
- What you are looking for in a university, department, lab, and or advisor.
