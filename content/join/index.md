### Getting in touch

***On the use of AI in communications:*** If you are contacting me, I want to hear from **you**! While I support the use and development of AI for many general and modeling tasks, the use of generative AI to read my publications, write your research questions, and draft an email will be both obvious and uninteresting. These emails will be ignored. If you write me a genuine email, I promise I will read it and consider you. 

Email [Ted Langhorst](mailto:ted.langhorst@uwyo.edu) with:

- The research questions or topics that excite you.
- What you are looking for in a university, department, lab, and or advisor. 
- Current CV.
