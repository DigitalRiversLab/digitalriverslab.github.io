### Getting in touch

If you are interested in joining the lab, please fill out the interest form. I review each response and will reach out within two weeks to set up a meeting if there is a potential fit.

<a class="btn" href="https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/5iHhA0NWZa">Fill out the interest form →</a>

Be ready to share:

- The research questions or topics that excite you.
- What you are looking for in a university, department, lab, and or advisor. 
- Current CV. 

***On the use of AI in communications:*** If you are contacting me, I want to hear from **you**! While I support the use and development of AI for many general and modeling tasks, the use of generative AI to read my publications, write your research questions, and fill out the form will be both obvious and uninteresting. These responses will be ignored. If you send me a genuine response, I promise I will read it and consider you. 

For other questions, email [Ted Langhorst](mailto:ted.langhorst@uwyo.edu).
